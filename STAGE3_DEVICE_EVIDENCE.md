# Stage 3 native-device evidence (deferred for the initial MVP)

**Status: waived for the initial MVP and Stage 3 Alpha Exit. No native-device
capture or pre-release participant study is required or claimed.** See the
[pre-MVP scope decision](forbidden_table_spec/STAGE_3_PRE_MVP_SCOPE_DECISION.md).

The pre-MVP Run Scene does not expose device-evidence capture controls. Do not
follow or refresh the legacy PC/Steam Deck capture procedure for this build.
The earlier 16.7 ms PC frame, 33.3 ms Steam Deck frame, and 100 ms
command-feedback targets are deferred planning values, not current acceptance
criteria or measurements.

Native-device evaluation may be reconsidered after the first MVP release if the
maintainer reopens that scope. At that point, revise this document against the
actual build, approved devices, and current capture implementation before
collecting evidence. Do not treat WSL2 timings as native PC or Steam Deck
measurements.

The invited human study is a separate optional post-release follow-up tracked
by [issue #68](https://github.com/sunnyday9/Forbidden-Table/issues/68). No
participant playtesting occurred for the initial MVP or Alpha Exit, and no
human result is represented as passed.
